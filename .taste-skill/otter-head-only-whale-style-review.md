This supersedes the shell/paws concept. In both panels, only the head, ears and whiskers appear above water. No shell, object, forepaw/knuckle form, shoulder, chest or lower body remains visible. Foreground ripples cross directly below the jaw and obscure the head base. The expression is calm and closed-mouthed. Ink-blue contours and grouped slate/icy highlights bring the drawing toward the actual whale's rendering language; the furred head is not claimed to have identical texture to whale skin. The generated water also has stronger painted marks than the preceding mockup.
