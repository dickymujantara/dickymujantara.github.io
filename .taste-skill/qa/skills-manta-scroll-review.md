# Final manta-only scroll checks

All **6 focused checks pass**, with no JavaScript errors, in [the results](skills-manta-scroll.json). These results and linked phase captures describe the final stationary-background implementation, superseding the earlier exploratory water/frame parallax checks.

The outer `.skills-manta` wrapper is sampled directly, separately from its already-existing inner ambient animation. Chromium at 1440 × 900 and 768 × 1024 advances through nominal 0%, 50% and 100% chapter progress, then reverses to the middle and start. The outer manta translation, rotation and scale change with scroll and return to their prior poses. Visible anatomy and both treatment frames stay clear of the text column; all 26 skills and both frames remain reachable.

The sandscape background has computed transform `none`, no animation data attribute, no inline style and no outer `.scroll-water` wrapper. Its computed background position and style remain identical across time and every sampled scroll phase. The heading and both outer treatment-frame wrappers likewise retain transform `none`: they have no new scroll parallax. Their inner frame/crop animations continue the previously requested gentle ambient motion and obey the shared Pause control.

On WebKit at 390 × 844 and 320 × 568, the Skills frame is taller than the available viewport. Both profiles use the Skills-specific natural-scroll fallback: `position: relative`, `data-scroll-static` present and zero extra pin travel. The manta is visibly on screen throughout three distinct scroll samples and changes pose before its timeline reaches the end. At 390px the sampled progress values are 0.6505, 0.7547 and 0.8598, with more than 164px of visible manta height. At 320px they are 0.6779, 0.7748 and 0.8708, with more than 131px visible. Reverse scrolling returns to the prior middle pose. All seven text rows and both frames are reachable on each phone.

Pause freezes the manta's outer scroll pose and all inner ambient transforms while the page scrolls; Play catches the manta up to the current chapter position. Runtime reduced motion removes the manta scroll transform, keeps the background and other outer wrappers still, pauses ambient motion and preserves all text. The five-sentence biography remains solely in About.

The first harness comparison accepted a relatively broad progress window before comparing subpixel poses; it was corrected to wait for the actual integer-rounded scroll target to settle. Animated-frame reachability now uses direct DOM scrolling rather than Playwright's element-stability wait. The linked JSON is the final clean run against the final source; neither harness correction changed the application.
